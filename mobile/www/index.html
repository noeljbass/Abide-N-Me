Abide N Me is a Bible reading plan you build and lead yourself — for your family, your small group, or just you. Choose the books, set the pace, and read or listen together.

Create a free account Read the Bible now
  • ✧

    Free, with nothing to buy

    No subscription, no trial that ends, no locked translations. Every account gets everything.

  • ◆

    No ads, no trackers

    There is no advertising and no third-party analytics anywhere in this app. What you read is kept so your group can encourage one another, and for nothing else.

  • ☗

    Led by a parent, not an algorithm

    You write the plan and decide what your household reads and when. Groups are private and joined by a code you hand out yourself.

  • ♪

    Read it or hear it

    Every chapter can be read on screen or read aloud by your device, so Scripture fits a school run or a bedtime as well as a quiet morning.

Prefer it as an app?

Abide N Me works in any browser. There is also an Android build if you would rather have it on your home screen, without a browser around it.

Download for Android

    Delete your account

    Removes your account and everything in it, permanently. Groups you own are handed to another member so their reading is not disturbed.

    Delete your account?

    This cannot be undone. Deleting your account removes:

    • your profile, username and picture
    • your reading progress and listening position
    • your notes, highlights and bookmarks
    • your membership of every group

    A group you own passes to its longest-standing member. A group with nobody else in it is deleted along with its plans.
